This archive collects my personal study notes on the mathematical foundations of stochastic processes, statistical mechanics, and molecular simulation. Written to deepen my theoretical grasp of diffusive transport, fluctuation-dissipation relations, and stochastic differential equations (SDEs), these documents bridge probability theory with atomistic modeling.

*Disclaimer: These are informal self-study notes compiled for personal reference. They are partly handwritten and partly written in Chinese, and they have not been peer-reviewed.*

- **[sde-brownian-motion.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/sde-brownian-motion.pdf" | relative_url }})** — Introduction to stochastic differential equations, standard Brownian motion (Wiener process) properties, and continuous-time random paths.
- **[outline-stochastic-calculus.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/outline-stochastic-calculus.pdf" | relative_url }})** — High-level structural overview of martingales, stochastic integration, and the core theorems of stochastic calculus.
- **[stochastic-processes-ito-calculus.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/stochastic-processes-ito-calculus.pdf" | relative_url }})** — Derivations and worked examples of Itô integrals, Itô's lemma, and stochastic chain rules for functions of diffusion processes.
- **[geometric-brownian-motion.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/geometric-brownian-motion.pdf" | relative_url }})** — Analytical solution, moments, and log-normal probability distributions of geometric Brownian motion under Itô calculus.
- **[quadratic-variation.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/quadratic-variation.pdf" | relative_url }})** — Mathematical treatment of total and quadratic variation of sample paths, explaining why (d<i>W</i><sub><i>t</i></sub>)<sup>2</sup> = d<i>t</i> in stochastic integration.
- **[random-walks-fluctuation-dissipation.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/random-walks-fluctuation-dissipation.pdf" | relative_url }})** — Connection between discrete random walks, Langevin dynamics, Einstein diffusivity relations, and the fluctuation-dissipation theorem.
- **[monte-carlo-and-molecular-dynamics.pdf]({{ "/assets/pdf/notes/monte-carlo-and-molecular-dynamics.pdf" | relative_url }})** — Core statistical mechanics concepts, ensemble averages, Metropolis Monte Carlo sampling, and numerical integration in molecular dynamics.
